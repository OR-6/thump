# Thump UI direction (18 Nov)

One look for the headset panel, the sign over the instruments, and the website. Think music hardware: a drum machine,
a mixer, a good synth's screen. Built by someone who cares, not generated.

## Rules
- **Surfaces:** warm graphite, flat. Panel `#161419`, raised `#211e24`, pressed/selected `#2b2730`, hairline `#36323b`.
  No purple, no gradients, no glows, no drop shadows on text inside the panel.
- **Shapes:** plain rounded rectangles (radius 10–14 px on a ~1600 px wide panel; 6–8 px on the site). No slanted
  parallelograms, no diagonal cuts, no angled ribbons.
- **One accent:** signal orange `#ff5a1f`, used for the one primary action on a screen (flat fill, dark text `#1a0d07`)
  and small "this one" markers (a 4 px bar, a dot). Never as a big wash.
- **Text:** off-white `#f2eee8`; secondary `#a29da8`; tertiary `#6d6873`. Upright, bold for titles, medium for body.
  Small uppercase labels with letter spacing for section names (like hardware silkscreen). Numbers tabular.
- **States:** selected = raised fill + orange bar at the left (rows) or bottom (tiles). Disabled = tertiary text, no fill.
  Toggles: a plain track; on = orange knob side, off = grey.
- **Level choice:** a segmented control (Easy / Normal / Hard), selected segment filled off-white with dark text. No
  traffic-light colours.
- **Judgements and score** (in play): Perfect warm white, Good soft green `#9fd98a`, Miss muted red `#ff6b5a`, flat.
- **Icons:** only where they carry meaning; the instrument thumbnails (renders of the real models) stay. No stock clip-art
  (no music-note glyph). If a line icon is needed: 3 px stroke, round caps, off-white.
- **Copy:** the owner's voice (see the my-writing-style skill): casual, direct, first person on the site, a "!" on the
  one or two things worth it, no colons-with-lists, no em dashes, no slogans in threes. Buttons: one or two words.

## Site
- A hackathon project page, not a startup landing page: what it is (one line), the video (placeholder until recorded),
  a few screenshots (placeholders), how to play (open the link in the Quest browser, sit at a desk, look down), the six
  instruments (playable drawing stays), songs (6 originals and 13 classics: write "originals" without a number if the
  count changes), play together, built with, who made it, the competition line.
- Same palette and shapes as the panel; light or dark both fine as long as the tokens match.
