# Drums: reference and measurements

Real kit, for proportions and materials: a 14" snare (35 cm, 12 to 14 cm deep, 8 or 10 lugs, a throw-off on the side, a coated head worn grey in the middle), a 14" hi-hat (two cymbals about 1 cm apart, the top on a clutch, turned lathe rings, a plain bell), a 20 to 22" kick (a resonant front head, often with a port and the band's name).

What's on the desk, and why it differs:

| Part | Real | Thump | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Snare | 35 cm, 13 cm deep | 13 cm, head 2.2 cm above the desk | the hit is the real desk under your hand (the thump); a tall shell would put the head in the air |
| Kick | 50 cm, played by foot | 15 cm, dark head with a port and your band's name | played by hand on the desk |
| Hi-hat | 35 cm, 1 cm gap, on a stand | 12 cm, 1 cm gap, on a small chrome tripod, top tipped toward you | the same |
| Spacing | | 4 cm between pads at least | fingertip tracking lands within about 1.5 cm |
| Whole kit | about 150 cm | 48 cm (63 with the tom) | a desk or an airplane tray |

Materials: shells clear-coated lacquer over wood grain (clearcoat 1, roughness 0.08 on the coat); heads off-white, roughness 0.8, a fine normal, a worn ring; hoops, lugs, rods and claws chrome (metalness 1, roughness 0.14); cymbals brass (metalness 1) with turned rings in colour and roughness.
