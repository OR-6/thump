# Congas: reference and measurements

Real congas: a tumba (about 30 cm head) and a quinto or conga (about 28 cm), tall staved barrels that flare at the belly, rawhide heads, black crown hoops with side plates and lugs.

Thump: two drums 15 and 13 cm across, heads 2 cm above the desk (the hit is the desk), the top of the barrel flaring out to the desk so the staved wood shows round each head from the seat, rawhide heads with a faint line where the edge (slap) begins, black crown hoops, chrome lugs.
