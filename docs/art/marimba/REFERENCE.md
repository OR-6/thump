# Marimba: reference and measurements

A real marimba: rosewood bars with rounded corners, graduated (the low bars long and wide), resting on cords through holes at their nodes (about 22% in from each end), the frame rails following the node line and closing in toward the high end, posts between the bars, graduated resonator tubes under them, mallets with wound heads.

Thump: eight bars 4.8 cm wide, 14 cm long at the low end to 8.4 cm at the high end, a soft edge, grain along the length under a light clear coat, on rails along the node line with cord and brass posts. The bars sit just above the desk (the hit is the desk), so the resonators can't hang under them: they lie behind, graduated, chrome. Mallets appear in a pinching hand.
