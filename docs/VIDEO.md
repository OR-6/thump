# Video: shot list and voice-over (real passthrough on a Quest 3)

Target **2:30** (judges may stop at 3:00). Real gameplay only, recorded in the headset. The first second is a real palm hitting a real table, and a drum.

## Before you record

- **Table:** a real desk or kitchen table, clear, warm light, nothing branded in view. A plant or a lamp at the edge of the frame helps it read as a real room. A wall within arm's reach (for the gong shot) is ideal.
- **Headset:** charged, lenses clean, hand tracking on, controllers out of reach.
- **Recording:** Meta button → Camera → Record video, landscape, highest resolution. **Microphone on** for these shots: the real thud of the table under the synthesised drum is the point. (We can lay the clean game audio under it afterwards if the mic is muddy.)
- **Move slowly.** Turn your head half as fast as feels natural; hold each shot a beat before and after.
- **Fresh start** for shots 1–4: on the 2D page open Settings → *Start over*. The address for filming is `https://or-6.github.io/thump/` (no `?debug`).

## Voice-over (short, warm; the hook by second 3)

- 0:00 (the slap, no words for one beat)
- 0:02 "This is Thump. Your table is the drum."
- 0:08 "Tap the pad. Copy the groove. That's the whole lesson."
- 0:30 "Notes slide along your real table. The crowd is your score."
- 1:00 "Every song adds to your kit."
- 1:15 "The gong goes on your real wall."
- 1:30 "And a little bandmate listens, and answers back."
- 1:50 "A friend's phone can be the bass."
- 2:05 "Move any pad anywhere: it's your kit, in your room."
- 2:20 "Thump. Play your room."

Feature chips (top right): **hand tracking** on shot 1–2, **plane detection** on shot 2, **passthrough** on 3, **scene understanding** on 6 (the wall), **persistent anchor** on 9.
