in minor thirds). License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0).
https://github.com/sfzinstruments/Discord-SFZ-GM-Bank

Changed for Thump: lead-in trimmed, the sustain loop dropped, each note shortened (about 3 s on the low E, under a
second at the top) with a fade, levelled, and encoded as mono 96 kbps mp3.
