The songs' backing tracks (public/songs/<song>/*.webm)

Rendered offline by tools/stems/make-stems.mjs from arrangements written for the game (tools/stems/arrange-stems.ts),
with FluidSynth (LGPL-2.1, used as a tool; nothing of it is shipped) playing:

  GeneralUser GS v2.0.3 by S. Christian Collins (https://www.schristiancollins.com/generaluser)
  Licence (GeneralUser GS License v2.0): "You may use GeneralUser GS without restriction for your own music creation,
  private or commercial." Its samples allow full use in music production, including recordings made for profit.

and every piano part (the classics' piano pieces, the lessons, the songs' keys) on:

  Salamander Grand Piano (a Yamaha C5) by Alexander Holm, in its SF2 build v3+20200602 assembled by the FreePats
  project (https://freepats.zenvoid.org). Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Changes: rendered as audio, mixed (EQ, compression, reverb).

The hall reverb's impulse response is made by the same script (no recording).

Scores the classics' arrangements are taken from (their notes, tools/stems/scores/*.json):

  Für Elise (Beethoven, WoO 59): typeset by Stelios Samelis for the Mutopia Project (Mutopia-2015/08/18-931).
  Public domain.
  The Entertainer (Joplin): typeset by Chris Sawer for the Mutopia Project (Mutopia-2016/11/25-263). Public domain.
  Rondo alla turca (Mozart, Sonata K. 331): typeset by Rune Zedeler and Chris Sawer for the Mutopia Project
  (Mutopia-2015/08/13-108). Public domain.
  Canon per 3 violini e basso (Pachelbel): typeset by Michael Fischer v. Mollard for the Mutopia Project
  (www.mutopiaproject.org, Mutopia-2015/09/02-2047), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes: re-timed to the game's tempo, its first violin's line taken
  as the tune you play, rendered as audio.

The other classics are traditional or long out of copyright and arranged here from the melodies in src/songs/tunes.ts.
The originals are the game's own.
